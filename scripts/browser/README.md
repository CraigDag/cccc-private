Browser walk-throughs against the preview (`scripts/preview.sh`, made-up data). Each prints OK or FAIL
per step and saves screenshots and PDFs to `$OUT` (default: the temporary folder's `cccc-browser`).

    node scripts/browser/every-surface.js        # Now, the plan: open, choose, drag, undo, add, draw, lenses, every PDF, Find; data, a register, cover in one press, download, a blank timetable's Start here
    node scripts/browser/keyboard.js             # Space chooses, Enter opens, Escape closes and focus comes back, Alt+arrows, Ctrl+K and / (and letters typed before Find has opened)
    node scripts/browser/two-people-and-phone.js # one person's drag reaches another's screen; the phone: Now, the plan, a drawer, Data, a report, Find
    node scripts/browser/a-coach.js              # a coach: Now's registers from earlier days match My week, everybody's registers, who is away, admin pages refuse
    node scripts/browser/scratch-week.js         # an editor: copy a class to the Scratch Week, plan in it (drag, add by day), copy a week in, place it onto a week, undo, empty; the phone; a coach never sees it
    node scripts/browser/hours-lines.js          # a facilitator's figure on Reports, on their week in the plan and on My week opens every class and time rule in it, adding up; cover alone; the phone
    node scripts/browser/student-hours.js        # Reports ▸ Student hours against the 15h target: meets, short (and why), a visitor never measured, totals, the overview's count; a student's week on the plan; a coach sees none; the phone
    node scripts/browser/next-year.js            # Administration ▸ Projects ▸ Start next year…: what it keeps and leaves, every semester in the same week number, the new year beside this one opening on the first week, this year untouched; no semesters, no year; the phone
    node scripts/browser/looking.js              # Look at it: next year in this tab only, the band on every page, the plan on next year's first week, a class added there unseen by the editor, printing and spreadsheets of it, a reload, Back, opening it for everybody, deleting it from another tab; the phone
    node scripts/browser/phone-calendar.js       # a coach's own semester for their phone from My week (only their classes, at their times, the day they are away cancelled), the phone; an administrator's for one coach from the plan, a class deleted cancelled in the next file, and erasing him forgets what his files carried
    node scripts/browser/former.js               # People & places: deleting a class, a facilitator and a space that were used keeps them former (a week that ran still shows them, the week ahead does not), Bring back, Merge into… with what it changes, the ended weeks re-pointed, Undo; the phone
    node scripts/browser/screens.js              # with AT=10:15 scripts/preview.sh: a TV paired by its code from Administration ▸ Screens, every space on it and nothing private, a class cancelled in the plan on it at its next look, a door sign on a tablet, Change… and Unpair reaching it, paging, offline, its cookie opening nothing else; the phone
    node scripts/browser/findtime.js             # with AT=10:15 scripts/preview.sh: Find a time for a facilitator and a group in a hall (soonest first, as the rule promised, Thursday's absence said), a time chosen filling in the add form with no clash, to Find a time and back from the add form keeping what was typed, Another time… moving a class in its week with its parts and Undo, Ctrl+K from a narrowed plan, an event booking its space in the add form, a coach offered none of it; the phone
    node scripts/browser/term.js                 # with AT=10:15 scripts/preview.sh: Plan ▸ Term, the semester's weeks side by side (blank, closed, this week), a week's classes and issues its own count, what is different from the week before (a change counted, a cancellation not, a copy the same) opening that class, Fill… copying the week before, Groups short of the target, People's hours for an administrator and counts for a coach, Ctrl+K and the arrows; the phone
    node scripts/browser/elsewhere.js            # with AT=10:15 scripts/preview.sh: a chosen class shades when its people, groups and space are elsewhere (the same on every row, never its own time) and lists it, dragging it says what it runs into (only their part for somebody in part of it) and letting go there moves it all the same, Undo putting it back, a line chooses the class in the way, a space shaded on groups' rows and said on spaces', a person's week shaded every day (Thursday away), a coach reads and drags nothing; the phone
    node scripts/browser/asking.js               # with DAY=wed AT=10:15 scripts/preview.sh (the sample week's Wednesday, any day): a coach's Can't come… saying first what it takes them off, nothing changing until an answer; Now's Waiting for your answer, soonest first; not this time, yes (an ordinary absence, its class needing cover) and yes to an offer; the coach offered the class left needing cover, taking an offer back and making it again, the offer first in the class's cover picks and pressed there; an editor answers nothing; the phone
    node scripts/browser/ways-out.js             # with DAY=wed AT=10:15 scripts/preview.sh (the sample week's Wednesday, any day): a class dragged into a space in use shows the clash and its ways out (two studios at the same time, then another time in its new space), one press taking one with the ring gone and Undo bringing both back, the other class's own way out (its teacher away all day, so no other time), another time saying where it was; an editor offered them, a coach not; the phone
    node scripts/browser/make-room.js            # with AT=10:15 scripts/preview.sh, any day of the sample week: on next week, Find a time for Finn and BA 1 in Studio B in the mornings finds nothing and offers to make room (Thursday moving Creation lab, Friday moving Contemporary dance), the add form opening there with the move ticked and nothing to warn of, saving both and one Undo taking both back, the tick taken off adding the class alone with the clash warned, the form saying the other class stays at a time it is not in the way, Another time… making both moves in one press; an editor offered the same; the phone

Each exits non-zero when a step fails. Set `CCCC_PREVIEW` to point them at another address.

They change the preview's data; start a fresh preview (delete .local-store-preview) between runs.
