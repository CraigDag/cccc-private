# What changed, for the person running the school

## 2.0.0-preview.1 — 2026-11-17

The whole app rebuilt. Everything the school uses today is here, with these differences you would notice:

- **It opens on Now**: the building this minute, like a departures board. Every space is a row saying
  what is in it and until when (with a bar showing how far through), or that it is free, what comes
  next, and a thin bar of its whole day. Beside them,
  what needs somebody today: cover, registers waiting for you, clashes, today's events. A coach linked
  to their name sees **their own day** at the top.
- **Plan shows a day with time running across** and the spaces down the side, so a free space and a
  double-booked one are obvious at a glance. One click turns it to groups or people, or to the whole
  week. Drag a class sideways to move it in time, onto another space to move it there, or stretch an
  end; double-click empty time to add a class right there.
- **Find instead of filters.** The box at the top (or Ctrl+K, or /) finds any student, facilitator,
  space, group or thing to do. Choosing a person shows their week, a day to a row; choosing a space
  shows its week.
- **Draw a class straight onto the plan**: press on empty time in a space's row and drag across it,
  and the add form opens with that space and those times. Adding a class says which spaces are busy
  then, and offers the free ones to press.
- **A class opens beside the plan**, not over it: when, where, who it is for and how many, who teaches
  and who is away, what is wrong with it, and Edit, Register, Cover, Cancel, Duplicate and Delete.
  Forms slide in from the right, so the plan stays in sight.
- **Cover in one press.** Beside every class that needs cover, on the Cover page and in the class's
  side panel on the plan, are the three people best placed to take it (free first, then the lightest
  week), each saying whether they are free and how many hours they have that week. Press a name and
  they have the class; Undo takes it back. Everybody else, and a note, are one more press away.
- **Adding or changing a class says who is busy**: a facilitator teaching another class then, or away,
  says so beside their name, the same way a busy space does.
- **Registers start with today's classes as big cards**, made to be tapped on a phone in the room. A
  class that ended earlier today says *not taken yet* rather than *to come*.
- **Now says when registers from earlier days are still to take**: a coach's own, matching My week's
  list, or for an administrator the school's, opening the list of exactly those, where pressing one
  takes it. A coach can widen Registers from their own classes to everybody's, and a class whose
  facilitator is away says so where the names go.
- **Two looks**: Paper (light, like a printed programme) and Night (dark), following the device unless
  you choose one in your menu. The printed sheets are in the Paper look too.
- **Print the building's day**: *Spaces, a page a day* is the plan's Spaces view on paper, every space
  down the side and the hours across, made to be pinned up. Printed for one group or person, every
  other class in a space is a grey bar, so a busy space never looks free on paper. A school with many
  spaces gets a second sheet for the day rather than a space left off.
- **A new, empty timetable says where to start**: what to put in, in the order the plan needs it,
  each ticked as it is done, or *Bring in a file…* to fill everything at once.
- **"Space" and "class" everywhere** a person reads, rather than "room" and "session": a Big Top is
  not a room.

- **Every change is kept the moment it is made.** There is no Save. Two people can work on the same week
  at once and each sees the other's changes straight away.
- **Undo takes back only your own changes**, in order, and says so if somebody else has changed the same
  thing since.
- **Every timetable has a history.** Administration ▸ Projects ▸ History shows who changed what and
  when, and can put the whole timetable back to how it was at any point (that is itself a change, so it
  can be undone too).
- **A week ahead, a week now and a week gone are all simply dated classes.** Copy week to… fills the
  weeks you choose and never overwrites a week that already has a register taken in it.
- **A group put in two places at once is one warning**, naming the students, rather than one warning
  per student. The same group booked twice is warned about even before anybody is enrolled in it.
- **Administration ▸ System says when the nightly backup last ran**, and turns amber after a missed
  night.
- **Erasing a person** (Administration ▸ Erase a person) removes their name from every timetable and
  every stored copy, keeps the counts adding up, and unlinks any account linked to them.
- **The morning register is here**: People & places ▸ Absences opens with today's students. Tap who is away,
  planned or unplanned, and pick a reason; their registers open with them excused, and a register
  already taken changes them from absent to excused (never from present).
- **Import people from a spreadsheet**: People & places ▸ Students and Facilitators take rows pasted from Excel,
  show what would be added (and who is left out, and why) before anything changes, and one Undo
  takes the whole import back.
- **Reports download as spreadsheets** (Hours, and Attendance) for whatever weeks are chosen, with
  each time rule in its own column and Æ Ø Å intact in Excel.
- **Finding somebody says where they are now**: under their name on the plan, the class they are in
  and until when, then what is next today (*Free until 15:00, then Aerial in Studio A*), or *Away*.
  A space says what is in it and who for; a group, its class. Press the class to look at it.
- **Now says what changed for today since yesterday** (since Friday, on a Monday): a class moved,
  cancelled, put in another space, given another teacher or cover, added or deleted, each saying what
  is different, who changed it and when. A change taken back again is not listed. So a change made
  late yesterday or first thing this morning is seen by whoever opens Now, not only by whoever made it.
  A coach's own day at the top marks which of their classes changed, and lists the ones they no
  longer have today (moved to another day, deleted, or given to somebody else).
- **A page left open keeps up with the day**: Now on a screen in the office moves on by itself as
  classes start and end, and turns to the next day after midnight.
- **Find opens what you typed**, even when Enter comes straight after the last letter (typing *Studio
  B* quickly used to open Studio A).
- **Register marks are thumb-sized on a phone**, and a long name no longer pushes them onto a line of
  their own.
- **The line at "now"** moves by itself through the day, runs beneath the classes so it never crosses
  their names, and the time sits on the ruler above it.
- **On a phone, the plan's buttons wrap onto a second line** rather than running off the edge of the
  screen, where *Week* and a person's name could not be seen; a person's week names its days short
  (*Wed 18*) so they fit.
- **A whole day of registers not taken is one line on Now** (*9 from today not taken yet*, opening
  Registers), so cover and clashes stay in sight; three or fewer are still a line each, to press.
- **Spaces and groups are listed under headings only when there are two kinds or more**, on Now, the
  plan, the add form and the printed day: a school that names no kinds of space gets no lone
  *Other spaces* heading over everything.
- **Bring in a file…** reads the current app's `.cccc` (every version) and says what came across.
- **It can sit beside the current app**, following its timetable rather than holding a copy that goes
  out of date: each save in the current app is here within a minute, so Now (with *Changed since
  yesterday*, which names *the current app* as who changed it), the plan and every other view show the
  school's real week with nothing typed twice. That timetable is changed in the current app, and this
  one says so wherever somebody would try. It is optional and off until it is switched on (the
  runbook's step 8), and it only ever reads the current app's file.
- **Erasing a person reaches somebody already taken off the timetable**, whose name the last 30 days
  of history still hold: they are listed under *Taken off in the last 30 days*.

- **The Scratch Week**, as in the current app since 1.67.0: one week with no dates, shared by everybody
  who edits, to try a timetable out in without touching a real week. *Copy to Scratch Week* on a class
  (or *More ▾ › Copy this week to Scratch Week…* for a whole week) puts a copy there and leaves the real
  class where it is. Nothing in it counts: no hours, registers, cover, or clashes with the real
  timetable. Plan in it as on any week, then *Place onto a week…* puts it onto one real week in one
  step, replacing that week's classes, and Undo takes that back. A week that has ended or has a register
  taken is never replaced. Coaches never see it.
- **It reads the current app's newest files** (1.67.0 onwards, with their Scratch Week), through
  *Bring in a file…* and when following.
- **Every hour explained**: press a facilitator's figure on Reports ▸ Facilitator hours (a week, or the
  total across the weeks chosen), *21h 30m this week* under their name on their week in the plan, or
  *How it adds up* on My week, and every class and time rule in it is listed a day at a time: their own
  part of a class, a class they covered (saying who they covered), and each time rule's credit, adding
  up to the figure pressed. *Classes*, *Cover* and *Time rules* show one kind and how much of the total
  it is. A coach sees their own; an administrator anybody's.
- **A student's hours, against the weekly target**: Reports ▸ Student hours lists every student under
  their group with the hours that count week by week, a total and their average week. The weekly target
  in People & places ▸ Settings (kept in the current app for its week tally) now measures each
  student's week, and a week short of it is marked, but never a week outside the semesters, a visiting
  company's, or a week in which nothing of theirs counted because the school was closed. Press any
  figure, or *16h this week* under a student's name when the plan is narrowed to them, for every class
  of theirs a day at a time: one that did not count says why (closed, cancelled, an open session, not
  counted), and a class still waiting for cover counts, as it does today. The Reports overview says how
  many students are short this week. Administrators only.
- **Start next year from this one** (Administration ▸ Projects) makes next year as a new timetable
  beside this one, which stays exactly as it was, registers and all. It lists first what carries over
  (the first timetabled week's classes, onto the same week next year, then blank weeks; the spaces, the
  people and groups, the catalogue, the rules and the settings) and what stays with this year (registers,
  every other week, this year's events and absences). Closures are a tick, off by default. **Every
  semester starts in the same week number next year**: the current app moves everything 52 weeks on,
  which after 2026 (a year with a week 53) would start next autumn a week early.
- **Typing straight after Ctrl+K is no longer lost**: the first letters of a name typed the moment Find
  was asked for could vanish before its box appeared, more often from home than in the building.
- **Copying a week again no longer takes a class out of another week**: if a copied class had since been
  moved to a different week, copying the first week a second time pulled it back out of there.

- **The end-of-day register reminder email** is here, off until it is switched on, with a Preview mode
  that sends every reminder to one address first. Administration ▸ System shows its state. The
  settings have the same names as the current app's.

Not carried over: Google sign-in (the school uses its own accounts), the floor plan (off the screen
since 1.31.0), and the old desktop apps.
